Keep this app

In your browser menu tap “Add to Home screen”. It then opens like a normal app, works offline, and your customer list stays on this phone.

Is this really free?

Yes. The app costs nothing. Messages go out through your own phone's messaging app on your normal SIM bundle, so there's no extra fee and no account to sign up for. WhatsApp messages use your data.

Why do I tap each person separately?

Because each message is personalised with their own name, your phone has to send them one at a time. It's about 2 seconds per person — fine for a few dozen. For hundreds at once, you need a paid SMS gateway (see below).

Sending to hundreds automatically

When you outgrow tapping, use a Ghana SMS provider — Arkesel or mNotify, around GHS 0.03 per message. They also give you a proper sender name like your business instead of your personal number.

I built you a server version that does this automatically — it's the smsblast folder in your workspace. You'd run it on a computer.

Message length & cost

One SMS is 160 characters. Longer messages get split and count as 2 or more. Watch out: a single emoji or a curly quote pasted from Word drops the limit to 70 characters. The counter on the Compose screen warns you.

Staying on the right side of the rules

Ghana's NCA expects promotional messages to let people opt out. Add Txt STOP to opt out to your message, and when someone asks, swipe them to Opt out on the Contacts screen — they'll be skipped from then on.

Avoid sending late at night. Between 8am and 8pm is the safe window.

Where is my data?

Only on this phone, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser data will erase it, so tap Save a backup file now and then.
